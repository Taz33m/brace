# Brace — pitch alignment and judge answers

**One sentence:** Brace turns an existing apartment walkthrough into an itemized, evidence-backed interior replacement-cost estimate, then extends it across the community using property records.

## Answers everyone should agree on

**What are you calculating?** The replacement cost of inventoried owner-owned interior fixtures, appliances and finishes. It is not the whole building's reconstruction cost, market value, an insurance quote or a promised payout.

**Why does this need an agent?** It must inspect multiple views, recognize items, distinguish repeated sightings and record uncertainty. Deep Agents delegates rooms to readers; the lead reconciles their findings. Arithmetic stays in code.

**Why all three data layers?** Matterport supplies interior observations and measurements. CoStar supplies property counts, floor plans and asking rents. Vexcel supplies exterior geometry and vegetation context for exposure ranking; it does not set interior prices.

**Did you inspect every apartment?** No. The current estimate extends a representative home across floor plans. Differences and unobserved renovations require verification.

**How accurate is it?** Sources and assumptions are inspectable, but we have not established professional-estimator accuracy. Unpriced lines are excluded; inventory uncertainty and broad price ranges remain visible.

**Is the fire image evidence of damage?** No. It is an illustrative edit. The inventory is grounded in the original interior imagery, and the replacement estimate does not establish which items actually burned.

**Is the agent running while we watch?** Initial screens load saved results. A requested room recheck invokes the agent again. Describe a recorded run as recorded.

**What proves the agent did useful work?** Open a real run's duplicate merge, exclusion or uncertainty, and its supporting view. Show the resulting inventory change; do not stage a correction as a genuine discovery.

**Who pays?** Our proposed buyer is a multifamily owner or manager. Assessment and update fees are a hypothesis; a customer pilot must validate value and willingness to pay.

## The presentation spine

Owner's question → interior item and source → property estimate and assumptions → one agent decision → usable paper.

Keep cleanup and refurnishing as a brief optional extra. End on the owner's output: **the total, with the evidence behind it.**
