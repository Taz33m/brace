# Brace — CoStar sponsor pitch

An apartment walkthrough helps an owner rent a home. We use that same walkthrough to answer another expensive question: **what would replacing its built-in interior cost after a fire?**

Brace sends AI inspectors through the rooms to inventory appliances, cabinets and fixtures. Matterport geometry supplies surface measurements. Separate research supplies price sources, and code calculates the replacement-cost schedule.

CoStar and Apartments.com records extend that one-home estimate across the community's floor-plan mix and show asking-rent exposure while homes are unavailable. Vexcel supplies building footprints and vegetation context for exposure ranking. These layers answer different parts of the same owner's decision: what is inside, how it scales across the property, and which buildings warrant attention first.

Open the property total and follow it down to a home, a room, an item, its visual evidence and its price source. The agent retains duplicate merges, exclusions and uncertainty so the owner can examine how the inventory was made.

**The new use of the data: a leasing walkthrough becomes a reusable financial inventory.**

## Show the judges

1. One building's aerial exposure context and supporting reasons.
2. One interior item, its location, quantity and price source.
3. The floor-plan assumptions that extend the estimate across the property.
4. The resulting paper the owner can review and share.

The figures estimate interior replacement cost. Other homes are extrapolated, and asking rents are not contracted income. The burned-room imagery illustrates the scenario; it does not measure actual damage.
