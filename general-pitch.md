# Brace — general judge pitch

Imagine a fire damages an apartment. Its owner needs to work out what replacing the cabinets, flooring, appliances and fixtures could cost. That starts with knowing what was there.

Now think of the virtual apartment tour you look through before renting. It already captures much of that interior. **Brace turns that walkthrough into an itemized replacement-cost estimate.**

We start with three kinds of building information: interior images and room measurements from Matterport; property records and apartment floor plans from CoStar, the company behind Apartments.com; and detailed aerial imagery from Vexcel, which adds building and vegetation context.

Our AI lead assigns each room to an inspector agent. They examine the images, record fixtures with supporting views, and reconcile duplicate sightings. Separate research finds prices, and code calculates the totals. Property records let us extend the estimate across similar apartments, with that extrapolation clearly identified.

Here is one item, where we saw it, and the source behind its price. Now follow it into the room, home and property total. The owner can inspect the assumptions and take away a readable report.

**The walkthrough that helped rent the home becomes a record of what replacing its interior could cost.**
